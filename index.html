You don't need to buy the guitar to find out you hate guitar.

Hardware, optional

HobbyBox: everything you need to start, nothing you don't

Boxes exist to remove the trip to the shop. They're an activation tool — the product is the discovery.

Boxes ship consumables for one real build, not a starter kit that outlives your interest. Expensive gear is rented through partner studios instead.

Offline

Take your hobby off the screen

Finish a trial and rate it "hooked" — nearby studios, walks and classes unlock.

Bookings are handled by partner studios. HobbyFlix earns a 10–20% commission on each seat, which is how the free tier stays free.
